The Song You Already Know

You've Heard It
A Thousand Times

Four kids met at Berklee, chased an unlikely dream to New York, and spent years playing to almost nobody. Then "Best Day of My Life" went off. Multi-platinum, a billion streams, stages all over the world.

Behind the platinum records were the same things that are in everyone's life: loss, failure, fear, unexpected change, starting over. Zac spent a decade singing those five words before he understood what they actually meant.

The Keynote

Make Every Day
The Best Day
Of Your Life

What if the "best day of your life" isn't something that happens to you, but something you choose to create?

Equal parts storytelling, humour, music and motivation. Zac uses the rise of American Authors, and everything that went wrong around it, as a working metaphor for the thing we all deal with: we can't control what happens to us, but we can control the mindset we bring to what happens next.

The Long Version

About Zac

Frontman and co-founder of multi-platinum band American Authors. Berklee College of Music to a career spanning hit records, worldwide touring, television, songwriting and more than a billion streams.

He's written with and for Carlos Santana, Rob Thomas, Michael Bolton, lovelytheband and Lit, including Santana's "Move" featuring Rob Thomas and American Authors, which reunited the pair more than two decades after their first hit together.

Today he's still writing and touring with American Authors, taking the keynote across the country, and building Bogoo's Best Day, a children's music project about positivity and self-worth whose first single, "Celebrate," hit #1 on SiriusXM.
